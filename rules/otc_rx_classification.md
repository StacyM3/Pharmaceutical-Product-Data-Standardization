# OTC vs Prescription Classification

## OTC (Over-the-Counter)

Medicines that can be sold without a prescription.

Examples:
- Paracetamol
- Supplements
- 

## Rx (Prescription Only)

Medicines that require a doctor's prescription.

Examples:
- Antibiotics
- Antimalarials
- Antihypertensives

## Importance

- Regulatory compliance
- Patient safety
- Proper dispensing practices
